About Me

My name is Tresford Mulilima, and I am a Computer Science student at Mulungushi University in Zambia. My academic interests include programming, web development, computer architecture, digital design and cybersecurity.

I learn best by combining theory with practical projects. Building websites and software allows me to understand how digital systems work and how technology can solve real-world problems. I am currently strengthening my skills in HTML and CSS while developing a deeper interest in responsive design, Java programming and software development.

My long-term goal is to create useful websites, applications and digital solutions that support individuals, organisations and businesses.

My Interests and Hobbies

  • Building websites and programming projects.

My Learning Plan

My approach to improving my web development skills is based on regular practice, reflection and the completion of practical projects.
