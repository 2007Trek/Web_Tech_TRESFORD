FIRST PRINCIPLES

Why I build while I learn

Reading gives me concepts; implementation reveals whether I understand them. When I work on a project, I try to break the problem into smaller parts, build a working version, test edge cases and revise the design when evidence shows a weakness.

This is the approach I want to keep applying to programming, databases, APIs and AI systems. These notes will grow as I publish researched explanations and lessons from completed work.

Published as an introductory learning note. Future technical articles will include code examples, references and reproducible steps.
